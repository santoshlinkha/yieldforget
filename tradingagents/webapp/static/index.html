          Backtest

          Forecast models

          Return forecasts made on this machine from Yahoo prices: no paid AI. A forecast is a log return above cash over its horizon in trading sessions, not a yearly rate, with a 5-95% range and the chance of a gain. These are model estimates, not outcomes; the evidence for each model is its walk-forward record below.

          Model comparison (walk-forward, out of sample)

          Each row was refitted every year on data available then, and scored on the years after. IC: how well the forecast ranked assets on each date (0 is chance). R²: whether its sizes beat "no return above cash" (negative is worse). Calibration: 1 if sizes are right, near 0 if far too confident. Coverage: share of outcomes inside the 5-95% range (target 90%). Net top fifth: per period, after 10 bp a side. p (Holm): chance the IC is luck, after counting every model tried. Feature importance is not causation.

          Risk levels

          Each level caps the stress loss, measured one of two ways. Worst case: what the whole portfolio would lose if every holding repeated its worst historical drop at once. Past crashes: the worst of the real crashes since 1998 (stocks, long bonds, gold, commodities) replayed on the mix, so holdings that did not fall together are not assumed to. In the 2008–2026 backtest it went past its limit by up to 1.5 times (docs/BACKTEST_RISK_MODELS.md), so worst case stays the default. Either way no single fund may use more than a quarter of the cap at its own worst drop. Level 10 has no cap; only the per-fund limits apply. A future crash can be deeper than any past one.

          With a growth target the plan takes the least stress loss that is expected to reach it, never more than your level allows.

          AI ratings

          Rates your holdings and the funds the plan wants to buy, then tilts expected growth. It never changes the risk level. Costs about one full analysis per fund.

          What to scan

          Your holdings are always included. Tickers with under 3 years of prices are skipped, since their crash risk cannot be measured. The best 60 scores go on to the optimizer.
