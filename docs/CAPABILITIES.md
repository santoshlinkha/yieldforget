# Robinhood Roth IRA capability matrix

**Checked 2026-10-23** against Robinhood's own help pages (sources below). Robinhood can
change these at any time, and its agreement says it may disable limited margin or
options privileges without notice. Recheck before relying on any row, and confirm
your own approvals in the app: this table describes what Robinhood offers in an
IRA, not what your account has enabled.

The app treats everything not marked **Allowed** as **research only**: it can be
studied and paper-traded, but it never appears in a recommendation for the real IRA.
The app never changes brokerage permissions and never sends orders.

| Instrument or feature | In the Roth IRA | Basis | App treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long stocks and ETFs, fractional shares | **Allowed** | Robinhood Retirement page: stocks and ETFs as in a regular account | Recommended |
| Buying with unsettled sale proceeds | Only with *IRA limited margin*; a cash IRA waits 1 day for stock and option sale proceeds | IRA overview | Assumes a **cash IRA**: buys use settled cash only (T+1). Switch only if your account shows limited margin |
| Borrowing, or a debit balance | **Not allowed** | IRA overview: "You can't borrow against the value of your IRA holdings or create a debit balance" | Never assumed; missing limits never mean leverage |
| Short selling stocks | **Not allowed** | Limited margin agreement: no "short selling"; short sales are for margin accounts | Research only |
| Options level 2: long calls, long puts, covered calls, cash-secured puts | **Allowed if approved** (enabled per account) | Options in Robinhood Retirement: "Approved customers can currently trade Level 2 strategies" | Research only until you mark your approval in Settings; then account-constrained (cash-secured, no naked selling) |
| Options level 3: spreads (vertical, calendar), straddles, strangles | **Not available** | Same page: "aren't available yet in Robinhood Retirement" | Research only |
| Selling naked options | **Not allowed** | Limited margin agreement | Research only |
| Futures | **Unverified**: no Robinhood page says futures trade in an IRA | Futures pages cover cash and margin accounts only | Research only |
| Crypto | **Unverified** for IRAs | No IRA source found | Research only |
| 0DTE options | Excluded by **your** rule (no day trading), whatever the broker allows | Your rule | Never recommended |
| Contributions | 2026 limit $7,500 under 50, $8,600 at 50 or over; prior-year contributions allowed until the tax deadline | IRA overview | Recorded as external flows; the app does not check the limit |
