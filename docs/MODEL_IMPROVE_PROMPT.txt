You are a quantitative researcher reviewing a small, real trading system. Below is the exact buy/sell
decision rule it uses today (taken from its code, not a summary), the account it trades, and the evidence
it has so far. Suggest concrete improvements. Be skeptical: the system has NOT beaten SPY yet.

=====================================================================================================
1. THE ACCOUNT AND ITS CONSTRAINTS
=====================================================================================================
- Robinhood Roth IRA, cash account (no margin, no shorting, no options). Settlement T+1: a buy can only
  use settled cash; sale proceeds are spendable the next session. No day trading.
- $29 is deposited every market day and bought into SGOV (0-3 month T-bills, ~3.6%/yr). SGOV is the base
  and is never traded.
- The ONLY money traded in stocks is the "trading budget":
      budget = SGOV dividends earned since the start + 3% broker match on deposits
               - money put into stocks + money back from stock sales
  That is roughly $35-40 per month, so positions are tiny (fractional shares; Robinhood has no commission).
- Universe: S&P 500 members (point-in-time membership since 2005; survivorship only partially handled).
- Data: free Yahoo Finance daily bars, SEC filings (fundamentals as filed), FRED macro series. No paid data.
- Hardware: laptop, RTX 3050 Ti (4 GB), 16 GB RAM. Everything runs locally.

=====================================================================================================
2. THE MODELS (fitted once a week on data up to the decision close; nothing after it is used)
=====================================================================================================
Notation: t = the decision date's close; trades enter at the NEXT session's close (t+1); H = 20 sessions.
sigma = daily EWMA volatility of log returns (lambda = 0.94) known at t;  sigma_H = sigma * sqrt(H).

Features (54, all known at t): cross-sectional ones are ranked within each date into [-0.5, 0.5]; market
and macro ones stay as levels.
  price/volume: mom_1m, mom_3m, mom_6m, mom_12_1, rev_1w, vol_1m, vol_3m, downvol_3m, beta_1y,
    dist_52w_high, gap_ma200, dd_1y, range_1m, log_dollar_vol, volume_trend, amihud, ma20_slope,
    ma50_slope, range_pos_20, range_pos_52w, rel_spy_1m, rel_spy_3m, rel_sector_1m, rel_sector_3m,
    volume_chg_1w, current_trend
  market: vix, vix_slope, tnx_chg_3m, irx, spy_mom_3m, spy_vol_1m
  fundamentals (as filed): rev_growth_y, rev_growth_q, gross_margin, op_margin, net_margin, fcf_margin,
    roa, liabilities_to_assets, dilution_1y, earnings_yield, sales_yield, fcf_yield, book_yield,
    filing_age_days
  macro (as published): cpi_yoy, unrate_chg_3m, payrolls_yoy, nfci, curve_10y2y, credit_spread,
    credit_chg_3m, fed_funds

(a) RANKER - XGBoost learning-to-rank (objective rank:pairwise; groups = decision dates).
    Training label: stock's 20-session return from the t+1 close, minus cash (T-bill) return.
    Relevance grade = the fifth (0..4) that label falls in among that date's stocks.
    Params: max_depth 3, eta 0.02, subsample 0.7, colsample 0.7, min_child_weight 200, lambda 10,
    rounds by early stopping on the last fifth of training dates. Decision dates every 5 sessions from 2006.
    Output today: raw score -> score_pct = its percentile among today's stocks (ties share the average).

(b) EXPECTED NET P&L MAP - from out-of-fold (yearly cross-fitted, purged) ranker percentiles to the
    realized net P&L of the exit policy's trade (section 3), in 10 equal percentile bins:
        expected_net(bin) = (n_bin * mean_bin + k * overall_mean) / (n_bin + k),   k = 200
    Also per bin: sd and 10th percentile of net P&L. Same map against SPY -> expected_excess_vs_spy.
    (Many stocks fall in one bin, so many share one expected_net; ties are ordered by score, then P(profit).)

(c) TRADE QUALITY - P(profit after costs) = P(policy trade's net P&L > 0): logistic regression
    (C = 0.1) on the 54 features + score_pct, then sigmoid (Platt) recalibration on the last fifth of
    training dates (purged, chronological).

(d) RISK - P(large loss) = P(position return at the 20-session exit <= expm1(-1.5 * sigma_H)), from
    XGBoost binary classification on the 54 features. (Terminal loss, not the worst point while held.)

(e) LIVE RECALIBRATION (new; inactive until 300 predictions over 10 decision days have matured):
    every day's predictions are saved and scored 20 sessions later (hold-to-deadline return from the
    t+1 close). Then P(profit) is mapped through an isotonic fit of realized wins on predicted P(profit),
    and expected_net through a line fitted on realized (return - cost) vs expected_net, shrunk toward
    slope 1 / intercept 0 by its standard error.

Cost: cost_rt = round-trip cost = bid-ask spread estimated from daily high/low/close (Abdi-Ranaldo 2017,
21 sessions before t) + 2 bp slippage per side.

=====================================================================================================
3. THE EXIT POLICY ("barrier_20") - used for selling and also to define the training target of (b)/(c)
=====================================================================================================
    entry E = close of session t+1
    profit level = E * exp(+1.0 * sigma_H)        (target)
    risk level   = E * exp(-1.0 * sigma_H)        (stop)
    deadline     = 20 sessions after entry
Rules, close-confirmed, never intraday:
- If a close is at/above the target or at/below the stop -> SELL at the NEXT session's close.
- If the next close would be at/after the deadline -> SELL at the next close (no extension).
- A lot is never sold on the session it was bought.
- If a position grows above 25% of (budget + stocks held) * 1.05 -> REDUCE it back to 25%.
- Net P&L of a trade = (exit price + dividends received) / E - 1 - cost_rt.
A separate ML "continuation" exit exists but is information only (it almost never fired in testing).

=====================================================================================================
4. THE BUY RULE (daily, after the close; orders execute at the next close)
=====================================================================================================
Candidates: today's S&P 500 members, sorted by (score_pct desc, P(profit) desc, ticker); top 25 examined.
A candidate passes the MODEL GATES only if ALL hold:
    P(large loss)   <= 15%          (missing estimate = fail)
    expected_net    >= +0.5%        (over the 20-session trade, after costs)
    P(profit)       >= 50%
    a current price exists
Then ACCOUNT GATES:
    not already held; at most 5 names including holdings;
    dollars = min(25% * (budget + stocks held), remaining budget / open slots); dollars >= $1;
    the new position must not make one stock > 10% of the whole IRA (SGOV included).
If data is more than 4 days old -> WAIT.
The app shows the first passing candidate as "Best buy":
    buy limit = last close (the model enters at the next close);
    "patient" limit = last close * (1 + median expected 20-session drawdown) from a separate signal model
      (about a 50% chance to fill within 20 sessions);
    target/stop/deadline as in section 3; shares = budget / buy limit.

Verdict labels shown per stock (same numbers):
    BUY   = score in the top fifth (percentile >= 0.80) AND expected_net > 0 AND P(profit) >= 50%
            AND P(large loss) <= 15%
    AVOID = bottom fifth (<= 0.20) OR expected_net < 0           (not held)
    SELL  = the exit policy says EXIT/REDUCE, OR (held AND bottom fifth AND expected_net < 0)
    HOLD / WATCH otherwise.
Warnings shown as context only (they do not change the decision):
    breakdown risk = P(trading below the 20-day low within 20 sessions) >= 1.2x its base rate and in the
    top fifth of today's stocks; crash = P(large loss) >= 1.5x base, >= 10%, top fifth.

VALIDATION GATE: real ENTER orders are allowed only after (1) a decision study on 2012-mid 2024 shows it
beats cash-flow-matched SPY after costs with a bootstrap interval above zero, beats cash, max drawdown
>= -35%, and P(profit) is no worse than the base rate in log loss; AND (2) a forward paper ("shadow")
account has >= 20 round trips and beats SPY. Until then buys are "PAPER ENTER" (experimental).
