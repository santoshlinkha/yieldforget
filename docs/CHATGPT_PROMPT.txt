## Method
1. Stress loss = sum over holdings of (weight x that holding's worst historical drop from peak).
   It is the loss if every holding repeated its worst drop at the same time. HARD RULE:
   stress loss <= my loss limit, measured on the whole portfolio value (cash included).
2. Worst drops to use (a longer-lived fund on the same index stands in for a young one):
   SGOV 0%, SCHD 33%, VXUS 36%, JEPI 50% (assumed), VTI 55%, AVUV 62% (via VBR), SPYG 68%,
   SOXX 70%, ARR 80%, TQQQ 82%, QQQM 83% (via QQQ), OKLO 85%, SOXL 90%.
   For any other ticker, use its worst drop since inception, and at least 50% if it is under
   10 years old.
3. No single holding may use more than 25% of the loss budget:
   weight x worst drop <= 0.25 x limit (skip this at level 10). No holding above 35% of the portfolio (SGOV excepted).
4. Expected return above cash = volatility x Sharpe, where Sharpe is a blend: 0.3 for every
   asset, moved toward the asset's own 10-year Sharpe in proportion to its years of history
   (at most halfway, for 10+ years). Then rank by expected GROWTH:
   growth = expected return - 0.5 x volatility^2. This charges leveraged and very volatile
   names (SOXL, TQQQ, OKLO) for volatility drag.
5. Universe: my holdings plus these ETFs: VOO, QQQ, VTV, VIG, USMV, QUAL, MTUM, RSP, IJR, VEA,
   VWO, XLK, XLV, XLF, XLE, XLU, XLP, XLI, VNQ, GLD, DBC, BIL, SHY, IEF, TLT, TIP, BND, LQD, HYG,
   UPRO. You may also suggest up to 5 S&P 500 stocks or disruptive-theme / small-cap names, but
   only with a stated worst drop and growth estimate. Skip anything with under 3 years of prices,
   and skip a fund that largely duplicates one already chosen (correlation above ~0.85).
6. Spend the loss budget on the best growth per unit of worst drop, subject to rules 1 and 3,
   keeping correlation in mind. Everything not invested stays in SGOV.
7. Optional tilt: if I paste ratings (Buy / Overweight / Hold / Underweight / Sell), add
   +0.30 / +0.15 / 0 / -0.15 / -0.30 to that asset's Sharpe. A tilt can move money between
   assets but can NEVER raise the stress loss above the limit.
8. Do not churn: skip any trade smaller than 0.5% of the portfolio.
